The alarm pierces the quiet at 6:00 AM, a relentless digital pulse dragging me from deep sleep. For a moment, the room is motionless, bathed in the soft blue twilight of dawn filtering through the blinds. Every instinct urges me to pull the duvet higher, to trade the coming day for another twenty minutes of warm lingering warmth.

Instead, momentum wins. I swing my legs over the bed frame, feet hitting the cold hardwood floor. The sudden shock of temperature clears the remaining haze. I stretch upward, feeling muscles loosen and joints align as the rhythm of the day starts to take hold.

In the kitchen, routine takes over seamlessly. The familiar click of the electric kettle signals the start of the morning ritual. As water heats, I open the back door to let the cool morning air rush in, carrying the scent of dew and damp earth. A single mug, filled with strong black coffee, delivers its first surge of heat to my palms, steam rising in gentle spirals into the dim air.

Then, sitting at the counter, I glance at the calendar on the wall.

A quiet realization washes over me: today is Saturday. There are no alarms required, no morning meetings, no rush. I slowly set the full mug back down, turn off the kettle, and lock the back door.

Walking back into the dim bedroom, I kick off my slippers and slide beneath the heavy duvet. The mattress accepts me instantly, still retaining the cozy warmth from just ten minutes ago. Pulling the blanket up to my chin, I close my eyes, letting the comforting dark settle back over my thoughts as I drift seamlessly back into sleep.
